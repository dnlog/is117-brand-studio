BRAND-001 / Logo proposal

A quiet, geometric wordmark.

This original SVG is a draft, not yet the chosen logo. It uses four open, square-built capitals to echo the modernist direction while keeping the name readable at navigation size.

Navigation size / ink

150px wide in the example header above.

One color / reversed

White paths on one dark field; no filter.

Small size / ink

72px wide to check the letter shapes.

What to inspect

  • Silhouette: four compact letters with a squared O and open joins.
  • Readability: the 150px header sample and 72px sample show how the strokes hold at two sizes.
  • Contrast: dark ink on paper and the explicit white one-color SVG on dark blue.
  • Risk: the technical geometry may feel generic or too rigid for Form; no trademark search has been done.
