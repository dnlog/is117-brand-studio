06 / Voice

Clear words.
Useful facts.

Approved traits: clear, direct, and understated. The example copy remains a draft.

Draft interface line

“Fabric composition: Not supplied.”

Say what is known and name the information gap plainly.

Sage in practice

Put useful product facts together and make the source easy to check. Do not turn missing evidence into a promise about comfort, quality, or sustainability.

A living reference. Change the shared tokens and logo, then compare this board with the storefront at the same width. Check the logo, typography, components, color roles, and voice text; sharing a stylesheet alone does not keep every example current. Save the reason for your choices in SPEC.
